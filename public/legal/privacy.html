Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 25, 2026

inPlace Care, LLC ("inPlace," "we," "us," or "our") operates the inPlace platform at yourinplace.com and the inPlace mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our services.

Plain English: We collect only what we need to connect families with caregivers. We don't sell your data. Ever.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and password when you create an account.
  • Profile information: Profile photo, biographical details, and role (family member, caregiver, or care recipient).
  • Care recipient information: Name, age, address, health conditions, medications, care preferences, emergency contacts, and care notes that you choose to enter about the person receiving care.
  • Caregiver information: Professional qualifications, certifications, work experience, availability, service areas, hourly rates, and background check authorization.
  • Communications: Messages sent through the inPlace messaging system between families and caregivers.
  • Visit information: Visit logs, care notes, and photos uploaded during or after care sessions.
  • Payment information: Payment method details processed through our third-party payment processor (Stripe). We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Feedback: Any feedback, reviews, or ratings you submit through the platform.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, and actions taken within the platform.
  • Device information: Browser type, operating system, and device type.
  • Location data: General location based on the address you provide, used to match caregivers with nearby families. We use OpenStreetMap for geocoding — your address is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to convert it to coordinates.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies, does not track individuals across sites, and is compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR. No personal data is collected by our analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the inPlace platform.
  • Match families with appropriate caregivers based on location, availability, and care needs.
  • Facilitate communication between families and caregivers.
  • Process payments and maintain transaction records.
  • Send service-related notifications (session confirmations, schedule changes, messages).
  • Conduct caregiver background checks through authorized third-party services.
  • Respond to your requests, questions, and feedback.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only in these circumstances:

  • Between families and caregivers: When a care session is booked, relevant information (name, care needs, address, schedule) is shared between the family and assigned caregiver to facilitate care.
  • Care team members: If you invite others to a care team, those members can see the care recipient's profile, schedule, and activity feed.
  • Service providers: We work with trusted third-party services to operate our platform, including payment processing (Stripe), email delivery (Resend), background check providers, and hosting (Railway). These providers access only the data necessary to perform their services and are bound by their own privacy policies.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of inPlace, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify users of any such change.

4. Health Information

Our platform allows families to enter health-related information about care recipients, including health conditions, medications, and care preferences. This information is provided voluntarily by users and is used solely to facilitate appropriate care matching and delivery.

inPlace is not a covered entity under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). However, we treat all health-related information with heightened care and restrict access to authorized users (assigned caregivers and care team members) only.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Password hashing using bcrypt.
  • JWT-based authentication with configurable expiration.
  • Optional two-factor authentication (TOTP).
  • Role-based access controls limiting data visibility by user type.

No system is perfectly secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you to use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or necessary for legitimate business purposes (such as maintaining financial records for tax compliance).

Visit logs, care notes, and session history may be retained in anonymized form for quality assurance and platform improvement.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Portability: Request a machine-readable copy of your data.
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@yourinplace.com.

Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA)

Virginia residents have additional rights under the VCDPA, including the right to confirm whether we are processing your personal data, access your data, correct inaccuracies, delete your data, and obtain a portable copy. You may also opt out of the processing of personal data for targeted advertising, sale of personal data, or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

To exercise your VCDPA rights, contact us at privacy@yourinplace.com. We will respond within 45 days. If we decline your request, you may appeal by contacting us at the same address.

8. Cookies and Tracking

The inPlace platform uses minimal cookies:

  • Authentication token: A session token stored in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) to keep you signed in.
  • Trusted device token: If you enable two-factor authentication and choose to trust a device, a token is stored to remember that device.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. Our analytics provider (Plausible) is cookie-free.

9. Children's Privacy

inPlace is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a child under 18 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

10. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the effective date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, providing additional notice through the platform or via email.
