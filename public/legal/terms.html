2. The Service

InPlace Care is an on-demand care coordination platform that connects families seeking care for loved ones (“Families”) with independent professional caregivers (“Caregivers”). The Service enables Families to find, communicate with, schedule, and pay Caregivers for in-home care visits.

What InPlace Care Is

  • A marketplace that matches Families with vetted Caregivers (see Section 5 for how vetting works and its limited exceptions).
  • A coordination tool for scheduling, communication, and visit tracking.
  • A payment processing platform that handles transactions between Families and Caregivers.

What InPlace Care Is Not

  • InPlace Care is not a home health agency, staffing agency, or healthcare provider.
  • InPlace Care does not employ Caregivers. Caregivers are independent service providers.
  • InPlace Care does not provide medical advice, diagnoses, or treatment.
  • InPlace Care does not guarantee the availability, quality, or outcomes of any care provided.

Booking and Scheduling

Families request care sessions through the platform. Caregivers may accept or decline requests based on their availability. A confirmed session represents an agreement between the Family and the Caregiver. IPC facilitates this connection but is not a party to the care agreement itself.

Pricing

Session pricing is determined by service type, duration, and the Caregiver’s rate. Prices are displayed before booking and must be agreed to by both parties.

Payment Processing

Payments are processed through Stripe, our third-party payment processor. By using the Service, you agree to Stripe’s Terms of Service. Caregivers receive payouts according to the payout schedule displayed on the platform.

5. Background Checks and Verification

IPC requires Caregivers to authorize and pass background checks before they can accept care sessions. Background checks are conducted by authorized third-party providers and may include criminal history, sex offender registry, and identity verification.

Limited exception for pre-existing relationships: in limited cases, IPC may, in its sole discretion, approve a caregiver who has an established, pre-existing relationship with a specific family to provide services to that family only, without a completed background check. Any such caregiver is conspicuously identified on the platform as not background-checked, cannot accept sessions from any other family, and must complete a background check satisfactory to IPC before providing services to anyone else.

While we take reasonable steps to verify Caregivers, no screening process is foolproof. Families are encouraged to conduct their own due diligence, including reviewing Caregiver profiles, ratings, and reviews, and meeting Caregivers before the first session when possible.
