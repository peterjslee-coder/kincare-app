caregiver services; Time records.

1.  Clients. Any individual or family registered with and approved by IPC through IPC’s platform who has been matched with and accepted by Caregiver for the provision of Services, including any such individual or family acting through an authorized representative, is referred to herein (each, a “Client” and collectively, “Clients”).

Upon Caregiver’s acceptance of a Client match through IPC’s platform, Caregiver shall coordinate directly with the Client or the Client’s authorized representative to confirm the scope, schedule, and location of Services to be performed. Caregiver shall provide non-medical caregiving services to each Client, which may include companionship, personal care assistance (such as bathing, grooming, and dressing), meal preparation, light housekeeping, medication reminders, transportation assistance, and other activities of daily living as mutually agreed upon with each Client or Client’s authorized representative (the “Services”). The Services expressly exclude: (a) any medical, clinical, or skilled nursing services; (b) the administration of medications; (c) any services requiring a professional medical, nursing, or other healthcare license; and (d) any services not expressly authorized by IPC in writing.

3.  Standard of Services. Caregiver shall perform all Services in a professional, courteous, and respectful manner, treating each Client with dignity and compassion. Caregiver shall at all times conduct itself in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, industry standards and any policies or guidelines established by IPC.

4.  Driver’s License and Vehicle Requirements. If Caregiver’s Services include transporting Clients, Caregiver shall at all times maintain: (a) a valid driver’s license issued by the applicable state authority; (b) a vehicle that is currently registered, has passed all applicable state safety inspections, and is in a safe and operable condition fit for transporting Clients; and (c) automobile liability insurance meeting the minimum coverage requirements of applicable law. Caregiver shall provide IPC with proof of any license, registration, inspection, or insurance required under this Section upon request. Caregiver’s failure to maintain any requirement of this Section shall disqualify Caregiver from performing any transportation-related Services until compliance is restored.

5.  Time Records. Caregiver shall accurately record all time worked in connection with the Services through IPC’s platform (the “Platform Time Records”). Platform Time Records may be recorded through the platform’s time-tracking tools or, where necessary, submitted as manual entries through the platform. Caregiver’s Compensation shall be calculated solely based on Platform Time Records. Caregiver shall not submit, record, or claim compensation for any hours not actually worked. Prior to any visit being deemed billable, the recorded time must be confirmed electronically by the Client or the Client’s authorized representative through IPC’s platform, or be deemed approved upon expiration of the review period displayed on IPC’s platform following completion of the visit (in either case, “Client Confirmation”). Caregiver acknowledges and agrees that IPC may capture geolocation data from Caregiver’s device at the start and end of each visit solely for the purpose of verifying Platform Time Records and resolving billing disputes or discrepancies.

6.  Time Records Audits. IPC may, at any time and upon reasonable notice, audit any Platform Time Records, Client Confirmations, geolocation data, or other billing-related documentation submitted by or associated with Caregiver (the “Audit Rights”). IPC may use such records and data for purposes including, but not limited to, resolving billing disputes, verifying time worked, and investigating suspected overbilling or fraud. Caregiver shall cooperate fully with any such audit and shall provide IPC with access to any records reasonably requested in connection therewith.

Compensation; fees.

1.  Compensation. In consideration for the Services performed by Caregiver through IPC’s platform, Caregiver shall receive compensation calculated as follows: (a) the base fee, equal to the hourly rate set by the Caregiver as displayed on IPC’s platform and agreed upon with the Client at the time of booking, multiplied by the total time spent performing Services as tracked through IPC’s platform, rounded up to the nearest one-tenth (0.1) of an hour (by way of example, if a Caregiver tracked two (2) hours and thirty-eight (38) minutes of Services, the time applied to the fee calculation would be two and seven-tenths (2.7) hours); (b) plus any gratuities processed through IPC’s platform in accordance with Section 7 of this Article; (c) plus seventy-five percent (75%) of any applicable Rush Fee in accordance with Section 3 of this Article; (d) less any applicable Platform Fees specified in Section 4 of this Article (collectively, the “Compensation”). All payments are processed exclusively through IPC’s third-party payment processor, and Caregiver’s Compensation is remitted directly to Caregiver by such payment processor; no Compensation passes through or is held by IPC at any time.

2.  No Expense Reimbursement. Caregiver shall be solely responsible for all expenses incurred in connection with the performance of the Services, including without limitation transportation, fuel, supplies, and equipment costs. IPC shall not reimburse Caregiver for any such expenses.

3.  Rush Fee. If a Client requests Services less than twenty-four (24) hours prior to the agreed commencement of such Services, a short-notice surcharge shall apply (the “Rush Fee”) at the then-current rate displayed on IPC’s platform at the time of booking. Caregiver shall be entitled to receive seventy-five percent (75%) of any Rush Fee charged in connection with Services performed by Caregiver, which amount shall be included in Caregiver’s Compensation in accordance with Section 1 of this Article. The remaining twenty-five percent (25%) of the Rush Fee shall be retained by IPC.

4.  Platform Fee. IPC charges the Client a platform fee in addition to the Caregiver’s agreed Compensation at the then-current rate disclosed to and accepted by the Client prior to accepting any Services (the “Platform Fee”). The Platform Fee is paid directly by the Client to IPC’s third-party payment processor. Any gratuities or tips processed through IPC’s platform in accordance with Section 6 of this Article shall be included in the gross amount subject to the Platform Fee. IPC reserves the right to modify the Platform Fee at any time.

5.  Expedited Transfer Fee. IPC’s third-party payment processor may offer expedited payment disbursements for an additional fee (the “Expedited Transfer Fee”). Such Expedited Transfer Fee is non-refundable and shall be deducted from Caregiver’s disbursement by the payment processor.

6.  Claw-Back Rights. If IPC determines, in its reasonable discretion, that Caregiver has overbilled any Client, falsified Platform Time Records or Client Confirmations or otherwise submitted inaccurate billing information, IPC shall have the right to: (a) direct its third-party payment processor to reverse or offset any payment disbursed to Caregiver to the extent of the overbilled amounts, to the extent technically and contractually available; (b) seek direct repayment from Caregiver of any overbilled amounts previously disbursed (collectively, the “Claw-Back Rights”); and (c) suspend or pause Caregiver’s access to IPC’s platform, pending IPC’s reasonable review and resolution of the matter (the “Suspension Right”). Exercise of IPC’s Claw-Back Rights and Suspension Right shall be without prejudice to any other rights or remedies available to IPC under this Agreement or applicable law, including the right to terminate this Agreement pursuant to Article I, Section 2. Any falsification of Platform Time Records or Client Confirmations, or any other act of billing fraud, shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement.

7.  Gratuities. Caregiver may accept any gratuity, tip, or gift of monetary value from any Client or Client’s authorized representative in connection with the performance of the Services, provided that all such gratuities are processed exclusively through IPC’s platform. Caregiver shall not solicit, request, or accept any gratuity, tip, or gift of monetary value outside of IPC’s platform, whether in cash or otherwise, and shall redirect any Client seeking to provide a gratuity to do so through IPC’s platform. Any gratuity processed through IPC’s platform shall be included in the gross amount subject to the Platform Fee in accordance with Article IV, Section 3.

8.  Cancellations. The following cancellation terms shall apply to all Services booked through IPC’s platform: (a) If a Client or Caregiver cancels a scheduled Service appointment more than twenty-four (24) hours prior to the agreed commencement time for such Services, the Client shall be entitled to a full refund of any amounts charged in connection with such appointment. (b) If a Client cancels a scheduled Service appointment less than twenty-four (24) hours prior to the agreed commencement time for such Services, the Client shall be charged a cancellation fee equal to one hundred percent (100%) of the total amount authorized for such appointment (the “Client Cancellation Fee”). Of any Client Cancellation Fee collected, Caregiver shall receive the same share such Caregiver would have received had the appointment been performed, net of the Platform Fee. IPC reserves the right to modify the Client Cancellation Fee and the applicable Caregiver share at any time upon posting to IPC’s platform, and any such modification shall apply only to appointments booked after such posting. (c) If Caregiver cancels a scheduled Service appointment less than twenty-four (24) hours prior to the agreed commencement time for such Services, or if Caregiver fails to appear for a scheduled Service appointment without prior cancellation, Client shall be entitled to a full refund of any amounts charged in connection with such appointment. The exercise of IPC’s rights under this Section shall be without prejudice to any other rights or remedies available to IPC under this Agreement or applicable law, including the right to terminate this Agreement pursuant to Article I, Section 2.
