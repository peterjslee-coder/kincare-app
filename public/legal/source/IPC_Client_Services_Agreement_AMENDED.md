RECITALS

IPC operates a technology platform that connects families and individuals seeking care with independent caregivers;

Client is either: (i) a Care Recipient seeking care services through IPC’s platform for their own benefit; or (ii) a Client’s Agent acting on behalf of a Care Recipient and seeking care services through IPC’s platform on such Care Recipient’s behalf;

IPC desires to provide Client with access to its platform for the purpose of receiving care services, according to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement; and

Client desires to receive care services through IPC’s platform, according to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.

Client services.

1.  Caregiver. As used in this Agreement, “Caregiver” means an independent contractor who has entered into a separate Caregiver Agreement with IPC and who is authorized to provide care services to Clients through IPC’s platform.

Time Approval; Payment; billing disputes.

1.  Client Approval of Caregiver Time. Following the Caregiver’s rendering of agreed upon Services, Client may review and confirm or dispute the time tracked by Caregiver through IPC’s platform (the “Time Records”) during the review period displayed on IPC’s platform following completion of the applicable visit (the “Confirmation Period”). If Client does not confirm or dispute the applicable Time Records within the Confirmation Period, Client shall be deemed to have approved such Time Records in full, and the corresponding Service Fees shall be processed automatically and remain due and payable in accordance with Section 2 of this Article. Billing errors identified after payment may still be disputed in accordance with Section 6 of this Article. If Client disputes any Time Records, Client shall submit a written dispute through IPC’s platform prior to the expiration of the Confirmation Period, identifying with specificity the time entries disputed and the basis for such dispute. IPC shall review all timely submitted disputes in good faith and notify Client of its determination. Notwithstanding any pending dispute, Client shall remain obligated to remit payment for any undisputed portion of the Service Fees.

2.  Payment. Client shall pay Caregiver and IPC, through IPC’s third-party payment processor, all amounts due in connection with the Services, calculated as follows: (a) the base fee, equal to the hourly rate agreed upon by Client and Caregiver as displayed on IPC’s platform at the time of booking (the “Agreed Rate”) multiplied by the total time spent administering Services as tracked by Caregiver through IPC’s platform, rounded up to the nearest one-tenth (0.1) of an hour (by way of example, if a Caregiver tracked two (2) hours and thirty-eight (38) minutes of Services, the time applied to the fee calculation would be two and seven-tenths (2.7) hours); (b) plus the Platform Fee as specified in Section 4 of this Article; (c) plus any applicable Rush Fee as specified in Section 3 of this Article; (d) less any discounts, promotional credits, or gift card balances applied to Client’s account at the time of payment (collectively, the “Service Fees”). Payment of Service Fees shall be processed automatically through IPC’s platform upon confirmation or deemed approval of the applicable Time Records in accordance with Section 1 of this Article.

3.  Rush Fee. Services requested by Client less than twenty-four (24) hours prior to the agreed commencement of such Services shall be subject to a short-notice surcharge (the “Rush Fee”) at the then-current rate displayed on IPC’s platform at the time of booking. Of the Rush Fee, seventy-five percent (75%) shall be payable to the Caregiver and twenty-five percent (25%) shall be payable to IPC.

4.  Platform Fee. In addition to the Service Fees, Client shall pay IPC a platform fee at the then-current percentage rate as displayed on IPC’s platform at the time of booking (the “Platform Fee”). All payment processing costs incurred in connection with the collection and disbursement of funds shall be borne by IPC out of the Platform Fee.

5.  Gratuities. Client may, but is not obligated to, provide a gratuity or tip to a Caregiver in connection with the performance of the Services, provided that all such gratuities are made exclusively through IPC’s platform. Client shall not provide any gratuity, tip, or gift of monetary value to any Caregiver outside of IPC’s platform, whether in cash, by electronic transfer, or otherwise. Any gratuity processed through IPC’s platform shall be included in the gross amount subject to the Platform Fee in accordance with Section 4 of this Article.

6.  Billing Disputes. Disputes regarding the accuracy of Time Records are governed exclusively by Section 1 of this Article and may not be raised under this Section. This Section applies solely to billing errors appearing on Client’s account, including without limitation an incorrect fee calculation, an unauthorized charge, or a duplicate charge. To dispute a billing error, Client must submit a written dispute to IPC by written notice in accordance with Article VIII, Section 2, within seven (7) calendar days of the date on which the disputed charge was processed to Client’s payment method on file (the “Billing Dispute Period”). Any billing dispute not submitted within the Billing Dispute Period shall be deemed waived by Client and shall be conclusively and irrevocably barred. A billing dispute submitted under this Section shall not excuse, defer, or otherwise affect Client’s obligation to remit payment for any undisputed charges in accordance with Section 2 of this Article. IPC shall review all timely submitted billing disputes in good faith and notify Client of its determination within ten (10) business days of receipt. If IPC determines that a billing error has occurred, IPC shall issue a credit to Client’s account or refund the erroneously charged amount, at IPC’s sole election, within ten (10) business days of such determination.

7.  No Expense Reimbursement. Client shall not be responsible for any expenses incurred by Caregiver in connection with the performance of the Services, including without limitation transportation, fuel, supplies, and equipment costs. Client shall have no obligation to reimburse IPC for any such expenses.

8.  Cancellations. The following cancellation terms shall apply to all Services booked through IPC’s platform: (a) If Client or Caregiver cancels a scheduled Service appointment more than twenty-four (24) hours prior to the agreed commencement time for such Services, Client shall be entitled to a full refund of any amounts charged in connection with such appointment. Of any Client Cancellation Fee collected, Caregiver shall receive the same share such Caregiver would have received had the appointment been performed, net of the Platform Fee. IPC reserves the right to modify the Client Cancellation Fee and the applicable Caregiver share at any time upon posting to IPC’s platform, and any such modification shall apply only to appointments booked after such posting. (c) If Caregiver cancels a scheduled Service appointment less than twenty-four (24) hours prior to the agreed commencement time for such Services, or if Caregiver fails to appear for a scheduled Service appointment without prior cancellation, Client shall be entitled to a full refund of any amounts charged in connection with such appointment.

No Direct Payments; Direct Relationship buyout.

1.  No Direct Payments. Client acknowledges and agrees that all payments for Services shall be made exclusively through IPC’s third-party payment processor. Client shall not make any direct payment to any Caregiver for Services arranged through IPC’s platform, whether in cash, by check, by electronic transfer, or by any other means.

2.  Direct Relationship Buyout. If Client and a Caregiver introduced through IPC’s platform mutually desire to establish a direct care relationship outside of IPC’s platform, Client shall provide written notice to IPC and remit a buyout fee prior to the commencement of any such direct relationship. The buyout fee shall be equal to the equivalent of three (3) months of projected platform commissions, calculated based on Client’s prior transaction history with that Caregiver through IPC’s platform; or, if no such prior transaction history exists, based on the then-current Platform Fee applied to the average monthly compensation earned by that Caregiver across all active Clients during the three (3) months preceding the buyout request, or such shorter period as the Caregiver has been active on the platform. Client’s failure to pay the applicable buyout fee prior to establishing any direct relationship with a Caregiver shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement.
