Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April ___, 2026

This PRIVACY POLICY (the “Policy”) explains how Cedar Rock Holdings, LLC, a Virginia limited liability company doing business as “InPlace Care” (“IPC,” “we,” or “us”), may collect, use, and share your personal information when you use www.yourinplace.com and the inPlace app available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store (the “Website and App”), in compliance with relevant U.S. privacy legislation, including the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA). Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal data.

Plain English: We collect only what we need to connect families with caregivers. We don’t sell your data. Ever.

1. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy at any time in our sole discretion. All changes are effective immediately upon posting. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and notify you through the platform or via email. Your continued use of the Website and App following the posting of a revised Policy constitutes your acceptance of those changes.

2. Data Controller

The data controller is Cedar Rock Holdings, LLC, doing business as InPlace Care.

3. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and password when you create an account.
  • Profile information: Profile photo, biographical details, and role (Family member, Caregiver, or Care Recipient).
  • Care recipient information: Name, age, address, health conditions, medications, care preferences, emergency contacts, and care notes that you choose to enter about the person receiving care.
  • Caregiver information: Professional qualifications, certifications, work experience, availability, service areas, hourly rates, and background check authorization.
  • Communications: Messages sent through the inPlace messaging system between users.
  • Visit information: Visit logs, care notes, and photos uploaded during or after care sessions.
  • Payment information: Payment method details processed through our third-party payment processor (Stripe). We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Feedback: Any feedback, reviews, or ratings you submit through the platform.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, and actions taken within the platform.
  • Device and technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
  • Location data: General location based on the address you provide, used to match caregivers with nearby families. We use OpenStreetMap for geocoding — your address is sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service to convert it to coordinates.

How We Collect Data

  • Direct interactions: When you create an account, submit forms, send messages, or contact us directly.
  • Third parties: We may receive personal data about you from identity verification services or background check providers when you engage with the Website and App.
Plain English: We collect what you give us (your profile, messages, care details) plus basic technical info (what device and browser you use). We don’t buy data about you from anyone.

4. Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies, does not track individuals across sites, and is compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR. No personal data is collected by our analytics.

5. How We Use Your Information

We will only use your personal data when the law allows us to. We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the InPlace Care platform.
  • Provide the services you have requested by signing up as a user (Family, Caregiver, or Care Recipient).
  • Match families with appropriate caregivers based on location, availability, and care needs.
  • Facilitate communication between families and caregivers.
  • Process payments and maintain transaction records.
  • Send service-related notifications (session confirmations, schedule changes, messages).
  • Conduct caregiver background checks through authorized third-party services.
  • Respond to your requests, questions, and feedback.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

6. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only in these circumstances:

  • Between families and caregivers: When a care session is booked, relevant information (name, care needs, address, schedule) is shared between the family and assigned caregiver to facilitate care.
  • Care team members: If you invite others to a care team, those members can see the care recipient’s profile, schedule, and activity feed.
  • Service providers: We work with trusted third-party services to operate our platform, including payment processing (Stripe), email delivery (Resend), background check providers (Checkr), and hosting (Railway). These providers access only the data necessary to perform their services.
  • Professional advisers: Including lawyers, bankers, auditors, and insurers who provide consultancy, banking, legal, insurance, and accounting services.
  • Regulators: Where we are required to do so by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify users of any such change.
Plain English: We share your info only with people who need it to provide your care, and with the services that keep the platform running. We never sell it.

7. Health Information

Our platform allows families to enter health-related information about care recipients, including health conditions, medications, and care preferences. This information is provided voluntarily by users and is used solely to facilitate appropriate care matching and delivery.

IPC is not a covered entity under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). However, we treat all health-related information with heightened care and restrict access to authorized users (assigned caregivers and care team members) only.

Plain English: Health details you share about your loved one are only visible to their assigned caregivers and care team. We’re not a hospital or insurance company, so HIPAA doesn’t technically apply to us — but we still treat health data with extra care.

8. Data Security

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way. Access to your personal data is limited to our employees, agents, contractors, and other third parties who have a business need to know. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Password hashing using industry-standard algorithms.
  • Token-based authentication with configurable expiration.
  • Optional two-factor authentication (TOTP).
  • Role-based access controls limiting data visibility by user type.

No system is perfectly secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you to use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

9. Data Retention

We will only retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes we collected it for, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within thirty (30) days, except where retention is required by law or necessary for legitimate business purposes (such as maintaining financial records for tax compliance).

Visit logs, care notes, and session history may be retained in anonymized form for quality assurance and platform improvement.

10. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you and any applicable regulatory authorities within the legally required timeframe.

11. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a minor without verified parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

12. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The InPlace Care platform uses minimal browser storage:

  • Authentication token: A session token stored in your browser to keep you signed in.
  • Trusted device token: If you enable two-factor authentication and choose to trust a device, a token is stored to remember that device.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. Our analytics provider (Plausible) is cookie-free and does not collect personal data.

Plain English: We don’t use tracking cookies or ads. The only things stored in your browser are your login token and (if you use two-factor auth) a trusted device token.

13. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or confirm whether we are processing it.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Data portability: Request a machine-readable copy of your data in a portable and readily usable format.
  • Opt-out: You have the right to opt out of the processing of personal data for targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA)

Virginia residents have additional rights under the VCDPA, including the right to confirm whether we are processing your personal data, access your data, correct inaccuracies, delete your data, and obtain a portable copy. You may also opt out of the processing of personal data for targeted advertising, sale of personal data, or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

To exercise your VCDPA rights, contact us at admin@yourinplace.com. We will respond within forty-five (45) days. If we decline your request, you may appeal by contacting us at the same address.
