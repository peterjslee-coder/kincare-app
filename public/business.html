inPlace — on-demand care for your loved one

Vetted local caregivers. Fair wages. Real-time peace of mind for families.

inPlace is a care-coordination marketplace operated at yourinplace.com. We match families with vetted local caregivers in hours — by the visit, no contracts, no agency markup. Caregivers keep 80%. Families see everything in real time.

Most families wait until a crisis to look for help. By then they are making rushed decisions under stress. inPlace is built around a different idea: arrange a few hours of trustworthy help before it becomes an emergency.

What we sell

Families book individual care visits. A visit is a defined block of non-medical, in-home support delivered by an independent caregiver the family chooses. We are not a nursing home and not a home-health agency; caregivers do not provide medical care.

How we charge, and how caregivers are paid

Pay per visit, not a subscription

Families pay one flat price per visit — typically $45 to $85 in total, depending on service type and duration. There is no hourly meter, no monthly minimum and no long-term contract.

Caregivers keep 80%

inPlace takes a 20% commission on each transaction. Caregivers keep 80% and are paid within 48 hours. Traditional agencies take up to 40%.

Payments are processed by Stripe. Families pay through the platform; caregivers receive payouts to their own bank account via Stripe Connect. Families can also record and settle out-of-pocket reimbursements between members of a care team — groceries, a prescription, a repair — so the household's care spending stays in one ledger.

Who uses inPlace

  • Families coordinating care for a parent or relative — booking visits, sharing a care record with siblings, and seeing what happened at each visit.
  • Care recipients who want a hand on their own terms while staying in their own home.
  • Caregivers — including nursing and pre-health students — who set their own schedule and rates, and build documented, paid caregiving experience.

Where we operate

Currently serving the New River Valley in Virginia — Radford, Blacksburg, Christiansburg and the surrounding area — and expanding across Virginia.

Safety and accountability

  • Caregivers complete identity verification and a background check before taking visits.
  • Caregivers check in and out of each visit, so families know when someone arrived and left.
  • Every visit produces a written record the whole care team can see.
  • Messages are screened for safety concerns, and families and caregivers can report or block anyone.
