InPlace Care — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

InPlace Care ("InPlace," "we," "us") provides a care coordination platform and voice companion service for families managing care for loved ones. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account, including your name, email address, and phone number. When you use the care coordination features, we store care schedules, session notes, and related care information that you enter.

When you use the Kindred voice companion feature, we request access to your device's microphone to enable voice conversations. Voice audio is processed in real time for speech-to-text conversion and is not stored as audio recordings. The text transcripts of voice conversations are stored to provide continuity within a conversation session.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate the InPlace platform, including coordinating care schedules, facilitating communication between care team members, processing payments, and powering the Kindred voice companion. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Microphone Access

The InPlace app requests microphone permission solely for the Kindred voice companion feature. Microphone access is only activated when you press the talk button. Audio is processed for speech recognition and is not recorded, stored, or shared as audio files.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate InPlace:

Anthropic (Claude) for AI-powered care assistance and voice companion responses. ElevenLabs for text-to-speech voice generation. Stripe for payment processing. Google Speech Recognition for voice-to-text conversion on mobile devices.

Each of these services processes data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure authentication, and access controls to protect your information.

Data Retention

We retain your account and care data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

InPlace is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@yourinplace.com.
