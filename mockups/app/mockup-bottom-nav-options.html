Bottom Nav — three ways to kill the white slab

Same six tabs, same home-indicator clearance rules, three treatments of the safe-area zone. The black pill is the iPhone home indicator. Pick one, mix them, or reject all three.

A · Tinted inset

The inset stops being "white space below the bar" and becomes the bottom of the bar itself — a soft tint gradient fills it. Labels stay where they are. Feels like iOS-native tab bars.
