Two things in here are deliberate, and both are easy to get wrong later

The team sees the visit, never the location. They see "Pete logged a visit" — exactly what they'd see if he tapped the button from his kitchen. They never see "Pete was detected at Betty's house." No arrival alerts, no presence, no location on anyone else's screen. That line is the whole difference between a nudge and surveillance.

Family visits are never blended into caregiver observations. They live in their own table and every report opts in, labelled. The failure mode we're designing against is a doctor report implying a nurse observed something a son did — the same derivation-chain problem as the v1.93 report.
